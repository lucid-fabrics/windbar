Your fan is three feet away. Turning it on shouldn't mean finding your phone, unlocking it, waiting for an app to load, and tapping through a screen.

Windbar puts your smart fans in the macOS menu bar. Click the icon, your fans are there. Click again to turn one off. Set a keyboard shortcut and you never have to click at all.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

• Power, speed, oscillation and mode, right from the menu bar
• Presets: get a fan how you like it, save it as "Desk" or "Night", and give it a key. One press sets the fan up and turns it on; press again and it turns off
• Every fan has its own Power key too, so one hits the bedroom and another hits the office, from any app, even full screen
• Ambient light, colour and display controls on the models that have them
• The fiddly per-device settings too, like Child Lock and display brightness
• Fans fold down to a single line each, so a house full of them still fits on screen
• Set up a brand new fan over Bluetooth, with no phone involved
• A fan that is offline is shown as offline, rather than pretending to work

BUILT FOR A KEYBOARD

Every control is reachable from a URL, so Shortcuts, Stream Deck, Keyboard Maestro, a foot pedal or any macro key can drive your fans. Copy a fan's or a preset's trigger link straight from its menu.

IT FITS YOUR FAN, NOT A GENERIC ONE

Windbar asks each fan what it can do and builds the controls to match, so a tower fan and an air circulator each get their own real options instead of a lowest common denominator. Newer models that keep their layout in the vendor app are covered too, with 84 of them described inside the app.

YOUR PASSWORD STAYS ON YOUR MAC

Your Dreo password lives in the macOS Keychain and is sent to nobody but Dreo. There is no Windbar account, no analytics, no telemetry and no ads. The app is sandboxed by macOS, and the complete source is published openly, so anything claimed here can be checked rather than taken on trust.

NO DOCK ICON, NO WINDOW

It lives in the menu bar and nowhere else.

BEFORE YOU BUY

Windbar works with Dreo smart fans and needs an existing Dreo account. That account must have a real password: if you signed up using "Continue with Google" or "Sign in with Apple", the sign-in this app depends on will not work.

Windbar is an independent project. It is not made by, endorsed by, or affiliated with Dreo.

Requires macOS 14 or later.
