The TurboCool Misting Fan 765S gets a Target Humidity slider, from 40 to 90%, under its mist
controls. Like the rest of this fan's extras, the range comes from how the Home Assistant
integration drives it rather than from our own hardware, so it is waiting on confirmation from
the owner.

Fans can now start and stop on a timer. While a fan runs, its card offers Turn Off After, from
30 minutes to 8 hours; while it is off, Turn On After. The countdown lives on the fan, not in
Windbar, so it still fires with the Mac asleep or the app closed, and the card's status line
shows when it will happen ("Off at 17:40"). The row only appears on fans that report Dreo's
timer, and the device report now says whether yours does.
