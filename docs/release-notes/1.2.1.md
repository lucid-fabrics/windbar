The ceiling fan's light now comes on at the brightness you asked for. The lamp accepts a
brightness sent right after its own power-on and then overwrites it while restoring its
previous state, so the room and the app disagreed until the next drag. Windbar now lets the
lamp settle before sending the value, on the ceiling fan and on the circulators' ambient
light alike.

The popover holds still too: expanding a fan, opening More options or the preset editor
used to make the whole panel shudder while it resized. And the shortcut recorders moved
under More options, where a set-once control belongs, so the card leads with the things
touched daily.
