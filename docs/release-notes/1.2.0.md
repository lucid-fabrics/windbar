The ceiling fan's light works now. The DR-HCF001S gets a Light switch with brightness and
color temperature sliders, in the device card and in presets. The fan was describing these
controls all along, just in a shape the app didn't understand, so it quietly dropped them.
Nudging the brightness also turns the light on first, the same way a preset turns the fan on.

A round of polish from the first feedback too: a collapsed fan expands from a click anywhere
on its row, the icon spins while the fan runs instead of pulsing in place and speed 1 shows
a filled bar instead of looking switched off.

Thanks to antonio1475 on GitHub for reporting both.
